Advancing Disability Inclusion & Accessibility in Nepal

Diverse Patterns Institute for Inclusion is a community-led institute co-creating practical, barrier-free pathways for civic participation, digital systems, and education across Nepal.

Explore Our Focus arrow_forward

Our Core Purpose

“Everyone deserves equal opportunities to learn, participate, access information, and lead.”
Focus Areas

What We Do

Inclusion & Accessibility

Advancing barrier-free digital spaces, accessible infrastructure, and assistive tech access.

Research & Learning

Conducting participatory studies with persons with disabilities to document lived realities.

Toolkits & Resources

Delivering open Nepali accessibility checklists, audits, and plain-language guides.

Awareness & Dialogue

Fostering active alliances between OPDs, municipal leaders, schools, and developers.

Our Approach

People with disabilities are primary leaders and innovators, not passive recipients.

01

Lived Experience

Programs and audits led directly by people navigating physical, sensory, and cognitive barriers daily.

02

Practical Solutions

Actionable, localized checklists and screen-reader protocols tailored specifically to Nepali digital platforms.

03

Shared Action

Enduring partnerships connecting grassroots organizations, government bodies, and civic tech teams.

campaign Featured Project

Digital Accessibility Overwatch

Practical training to help OPD leaders, educators, and local government officials in Lumbini and Madhesh understand digital accessibility and identify common barriers.

Who We Work With

Sustainable accessibility requires co-ownership across civil society.

OPDs & Advocates

Peer-to-peer partnerships across provincial disabled people's organizations.

Communities & Families

Listening to self-advocates, youth, and caregivers navigating access daily.

Schools & Universities

Equipping educators and students with accessible learning materials.

Public Bodies & Municipalities

Auditing citizen portals, public services, and building statutory standards.

Development Partners

Integrating disability inclusion directly into social development initiatives.

Tech Teams & Researchers

Collaborating with software designers, translators, and NSL specialists.
