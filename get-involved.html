Join Us in Building an Inclusive Society

Building an inclusive society requires cooperation, genuine listening, and shared commitment. We welcome individuals and organizations who want to contribute to disability inclusion and accessibility in Nepal.

How You Can Get Involved

We actively invite partnerships, shared learning, community exchange, and grassroots support across four main pathways.

diversity_1

1. Collaborating With Us

Work with us on research, awareness, training, and inclusion initiatives. We collaborate with OPDs, grassroots networks, universities, and public institutions to identify barriers and develop sustainable solutions.

share

2. Sharing Knowledge

Contribute your lived experience, technical ideas, and resources to promote accessibility. Lived experience is central to our work; your insights help ensure practical relevance.

volunteer_activism

3. Supporting Our Work

Help us develop and share open knowledge resources, support community field workshops, and back activities that promote equal participation across Nepal.

front_hand

4. Volunteering

Contribute your time and specialized skills, such as sign language interpretation, accessible web development, graphic design, document translation, or event facilitation.
